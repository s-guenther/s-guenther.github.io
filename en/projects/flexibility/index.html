Assessing flexibility potential

Demand-side management

How much load can be shifted in time, and what is it worth? For VEA I developed a tool that answers this question quantitatively, and ran the analyses with it.

Shifting electricity demand in time can lower load peaks and make use of cheaper price periods. How large this potential really is in a given operation is hard to estimate without a model.

  • Setting: freelance assignment
  • Client: VEA (Bundesverband der Energie-Abnehmer e. V., German association of energy consumers)
  • Tools: Python

My contribution

  • Development of a tool to assess load-shifting potential (demand-side management)
  • Modular optimisation model that generates itself from a block diagram
  • Running and evaluating the analyses
  • Preparing the results for the client

Outcome

  • A quantitative basis for decisions on using flexibility
  • Potential assessment for typical use cases
