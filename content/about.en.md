I am a mechatronics engineer and have worked in research and development on
energy systems and energy storage for more than ten years. Since 2024 I have
been working as a freelancer in Hannover.

My strength is the combination of engineering, mathematics and programming:
I formulate a technical problem as a model, implement it in Python and
evaluate the results with sound statistics. My studies and industry work add
the practical side: mechanical design, drives and measurement.

My dissertation on hybrid energy storage systems at Leibniz University
Hannover is submitted and graded. The defence takes place at the end of
November 2026.

## Background

**Freelance engineer, Hannover.** Modelling, simulation, optimisation and
software development for companies, with a focus on energy systems and
energy storage.

**Leibniz University Hannover, Institute of Electric Power Systems.**
Research associate in the Electrical Energy Storage Systems group. Research
and dissertation on hybrid energy storage systems (*Hybrid energy storage
systems: a generalized quantitative analysis with a focus on system
sizing*), work in the BMBF research project MetroHESS, an industry
assignment on storage sizing for wind farms, an energy concept with
ecovillage hannover eG, construction and laboratory supervision of a
flywheel storage system, teaching and thesis supervision, 2 × 12 months of parental leave.

**TII Energy GmbH & Co. KG, Dessau-Roßlau.** Research and development on
flywheel energy storage: finite element models for the strength of CFRP
rotors, multibody simulation, simulation of storage-supported rail networks.

**Studies in mechatronics, Otto von Guericke University Magdeburg.**
Bachelor and Master of Science with a focus on mechanics, robotics and
simulation. Industrial internship at Continental in Hannover in noise,
vibration and harshness (NVH).
