For structural health monitoring research on cylindrical parts made of
carbon-fibre-reinforced plastic, the measurement of wave propagation with a
laser scanning vibrometer was to be automated.

I designed and built a numerically controlled rotary table (shaft, bearings,
gearing, slip ring) and connected the stepper motor control to the Polytec
measurement software, so that the table turns the part in defined angular
steps and triggers a measurement after each step.
