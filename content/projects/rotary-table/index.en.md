- **Setting:** research on structural health monitoring, Otto von Guericke
  University Magdeburg
- **Tools:** 3D CAD, stepper motor control, Polytec measurement software

### My contribution

- Design and construction: shaft, bearings, gearing, slip ring
- Selection and programming of the stepper motor control
- Connection to the vibrometer software: rotation in defined angular steps,
  a measurement after each step

### Outcome

- Fully automated measurement cycles around the whole circumference instead
  of manual repositioning
- A low-cost setup that can be extended with further axes
