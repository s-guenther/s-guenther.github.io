I answer technical and economic questions with mathematical models, for
example:

- Which storage size, technology and operating strategy suit a given load or
  generation profile?
- How much load can be shifted in time, and what is it worth?
- What do existing measurement, operating and time-series data show?

For this I formulate optimisation problems (linear and nonlinear), simulate
operating strategies and run parameter studies with many variants, on a
computing cluster if needed.

**Tools:** Python, Pyomo, GLPK, IPOPT, pygmo, statistics and time-series
analysis, SLURM.

Examples: [assessing flexibility potential]({{< ref "projects/flexibility" >}}),
[hybrid energy storage systems]({{< ref "projects/dissertation" >}}),
[energy-plus neighbourhood]({{< ref "projects/residential-area" >}}).
