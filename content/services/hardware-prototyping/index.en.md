When an idea has to be tested on real hardware, I design and build
prototypes and test rigs: mechanics, drive, control and the connection to
measurement equipment.

- Mechanical design with 3D model and drawings; sizing of bearings, drives
  and gears.
- Estimating thermal behaviour and rotor dynamics.
- Motor control and automation of measurement sequences.

Examples: [flywheel storage prototype]({{< ref "projects/flywheel" >}}),
[NC rotary table for a laser scanning vibrometer]({{< ref "projects/rotary-table" >}}).
